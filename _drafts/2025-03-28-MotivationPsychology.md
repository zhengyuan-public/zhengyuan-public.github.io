## How is it possible?

For psychology, things are not simple as laws in physics. What motivates a person at one time may not do so at another time. And, what motivates one person may not motivate another. How is it possible to develop a psychology for motivation?

> Psychologists attempt to show how motivation varies **within** a person at different times or **among** different people at the same time.
{: .prompt-tip }

## Categories of Motivation

- Motive: A motive is a person’s relatively stable internal disposition to be concerned with and approach positive incentives and avoid negative incentives.
    - People’s goals can stem from their motives. The goal of a motive is the satisfaction of that motive
- Goal: A goal is represented as the internal image of a future outcome that a person plans to achieve: an end-state.
- Incentive: An incentive is an anticipated reward or aversive event available in the environment.
    - Incentive is contingent on behavior, which means that a person must perform the prescribed behavior to attain or avoid it.
